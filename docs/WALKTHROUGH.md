# GUTECH portal walkthrough (about 3 minutes)

Use this as the narration outline for the presentation video required by the project brief. Record the actual deployed site after production setup; keep account passwords and provider settings off-screen.

1. **Introduction (15 seconds):** “This is the GUTECH Student and Faculty Portal, built with React, Node.js, and Supabase.” Show the sign-in page and maroon branding.
2. **Admin (45 seconds):** Sign in as the admin. Show active teacher, enrolled student, and course counts. Open People and Courses, then show assignment/exam setup, gradebook, and announcements.
3. **Teacher (45 seconds):** Sign in as a teacher. Open a class, show the student count, attendance, assignment submissions, quizzes/exams, and marks.
4. **Student (35 seconds):** Sign in as a student. Show courses, assignments, quizzes/exams, marks, and announcements.
5. **Finish (20 seconds):** Show the sign-out control and state where the public repository and live site are hosted.

Do not record or publish the credentials workbook, service-role key, browser autofill, or private Supabase settings.
